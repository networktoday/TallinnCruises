Your guide monitors your ship's departure schedule throughout the entire tour. We build buffer time into every itinerary and always return you to the terminal at least 45 minutes before all-aboard time. In over 22 years of operation, we have never caused a guest to miss their ship.

It depends on the tour. Our Classic Essential walking tour involves about 3–4 km at an easy pace with frequent stops. Tours with vehicle transfers (Signature Tour, Most Complete) have even less walking since we drive between areas. We always adapt to your comfort level.

Tours run rain or shine — Tallinn is beautiful in all weather. If it rains, we shift to more indoor time (cafés, churches, covered passages) and use vehicle transfers. We never cancel due to rain.
